## Selection & comparison

A podcast tool fits this model when a recurring allowance funds availability but heavy users create extra processing cost. Flat pricing keeps one bundle price; pure usage pricing can start at zero. The base and overage must both be visible before use.

## Applications

The customer pays 20 monthly including 100 transcript exports, plus 0.10 per excess export. At 120 the total is 22; at zero or 100 it remains 20. Three seats are context, not a multiplier.

## Implementation & cautions

Show remaining allowance, overage rate and spending controls. Define whether unused allowance expires or rolls over; this demo uses a single month without rollover. Validate metering and billing separately from access and seller responsibilities.
