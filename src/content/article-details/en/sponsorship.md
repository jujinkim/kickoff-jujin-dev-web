## Selection & comparison

A public garden guide fits support from people who want the resource maintained. Direct payment would sell access; advertising would sell placements. Sponsorship may include acknowledgement, but does not inherently promise ad impressions or exclusive features.

## Applications

A supporter contributes 12 toward maintenance; every reader keeps access. This example promises acknowledgement only. One-time or recurring collection and the party issuing receipts must be stated separately.

## Implementation & cautions

Disclose benefits and editorial influence. Support is not automatically a charitable donation or tax deduction. Provider eligibility, fees and payout terms vary; the diagram neither transfers funds nor verifies eligibility.
