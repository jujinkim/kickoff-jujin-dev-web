## Selection & comparison

Choose reduction when secondary content competes with the primary task. Flat design removes simulated depth; it need not reduce information. A sparse page still needs clear navigation and recognizable controls.

## Applications

The hiking list puts essentials first and keeps comforts in a native disclosure. Reading pages and focused checklists can use the same approach. Three items is a demonstration choice, not a recommended complete hiking kit.

## Implementation & cautions

Use native checkboxes and details so labels and disclosure remain familiar. Do not hide required content merely to reduce page height. Test the unchecked, partly packed and fully packed states, keyboard focus, and the unenhanced page.
