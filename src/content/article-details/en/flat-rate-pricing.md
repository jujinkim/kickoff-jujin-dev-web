## Selection & comparison

A meal planner fits a single bundle when households prefer predictable cost. Per-seat pricing scales with licensed people; feature tiers separate capabilities. Flat pricing can include limits and does not mean unlimited consumption.

## Applications

The customer pays 20 per month for up to five seats and 200 exports. The baseline of three seats and 120 exports stays inside that bundle. The seller must explain what happens beyond either cap.

## Implementation & cautions

Show period, included capacity and restrictions together. Reject invalid quantities without silently changing the offer. Overages, proration, taxes and refunds are excluded here; a displayed monthly quote is not a completed charge.
