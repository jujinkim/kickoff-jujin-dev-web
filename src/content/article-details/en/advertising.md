## Selection & comparison

A cooking blog can keep recipes open while advertisers buy placements. Direct payment suits readers willing to buy access; sponsorship suits support tied to the publication rather than individual ad delivery. Advertising can coexist with either.

## Applications

Advertiser money reaches the publisher through an ad service. Readers receive recipes without paying in this example. Settlement timing, rate and valid-impression rules depend on the ad agreement; no earnings amount is simulated.

## Implementation & cautions

Label placements, protect reading space and define consent where required. Measure loading cost and invalid traffic. The diagram loads no ad network and does not demonstrate targeting, consent compliance or a guaranteed payout.
