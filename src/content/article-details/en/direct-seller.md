## Selection & comparison

A language lesson business may retain the customer sales contract to control its offer and relationship. A merchant of record can take agreed seller duties for supported products and markets. Direct selling preserves control while requiring operational capacity.

## Applications

The learner pays for a lesson license from the business. The business is the seller for receipts, refunds and transaction-tax duties in this diagram; a processor handles payment. Price, renewal and entitlement duration are independent choices.

## Implementation & cautions

Validate seller status, tax scope, consumer terms and dispute handling for the actual contract and jurisdiction. Do not infer liability from a payment button or logo. This responsibility map is not a complete legal or accounting assessment.
