## Selection & comparison

A music practice app can choose a merchant of record to delegate agreed transaction duties across supported markets. Direct selling fits a team that needs the sales contract and can operate it. Compare coverage, fees and control, rather than assuming all intermediaries become sellers.

## Applications

The learner buys the practice license from the merchant of record, which later settles with the app business. The diagram assigns receipts, refunds and transaction-tax duties to that seller. The app business continues product delivery and support.

## Implementation & cautions

Check the actual contract, supported countries, refund authority, reserves and payout timing. Transaction-tax coverage does not erase income tax or product obligations. Pricing and access must still be specified; the diagram performs no payment or settlement.
