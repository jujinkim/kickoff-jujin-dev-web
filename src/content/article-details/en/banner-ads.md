## Selection & comparison

A number puzzle can keep play visible above a reserved ad slot. Interstitials suit genuine transition points; rewarded ads require an optional exchange for a benefit. A banner should not masquerade as a puzzle control.

## Applications

The reader plays for free while an advertiser would fund the labeled bottom placement. The example has no network, rate or reward. The publisher remains responsible for positioning and the product experience under the ad agreement.

## Implementation & cautions

Reserve dimensions before loading to avoid layout shifts. Keep controls and ads separated, and handle no-fill without blocking play. This static mockup demonstrates space allocation, not ad delivery or policy approval.
