## Selection & comparison

Choose graphic emphasis for a small set of expressive, clearly grouped choices. Brutalism exposes a raw structure; this treatment deliberately builds polished graphic blocks. Neither requires broken interaction conventions.

## Applications

The fair uses a program card per activity and an explicit interest toggle. Promotions and cultural events can use this voice. Keep the schedule visible when an activity is selected; decoration should not replace the information.

## Implementation & cautions

Track selection independently from filtering. Use pressed semantics plus a check mark, and let cards wrap to one column. Shadows need space at narrow widths; they must not be the only boundary or focus signal.
