## Selection & comparison

Use expressive emphasis when one action should attract attention and the product voice permits it. Flat design can coexist with this approach; a restrained interface may need less shape variation and motion.

## Applications

The picnic separates invitation, group size and participation state. The curved action changes meaning visibly when joined. The flower is decorative; it does not carry the only confirmation signal.

## Implementation & cautions

Keep state text and pressed semantics independent of animation. Reduced motion removes the reaction. A real event needs server capacity checks; this local example accepts one to six places without making a reservation.
