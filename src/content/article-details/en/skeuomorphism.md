## Selection & comparison

Choose an object metaphor when people already understand its organization. A notebook supports recipes and tabs; a decorative leather surface alone does not teach navigation. Flat surfaces suit tasks where the metaphor would add little.

## Applications

The notebook changes both the selected dish and calculated quantities. Cooking, note-taking and audio tools can borrow meaningful physical relationships. Do not imitate an object so literally that useful digital operations become difficult.

## Implementation & cautions

Keep per-serving quantities separate from the display and recompute from the chosen serving count. Preserve units, readable page contrast and a single mobile reading order. The generated food image and recipe quantities are illustrative, not a tested recipe.
