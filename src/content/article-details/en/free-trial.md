## Selection & comparison

A team planner fits a trial when teams need to evaluate editing and export together before deciding to pay. Freemium keeps a useful free tier indefinitely. A product can combine a free tier with a trial of paid capabilities.

## Applications

Editing and PDF export work before day three, then lock. An explicit simulated paid choice restores both. This three-day policy does not auto-charge; price, paid period and the seller contract are outside the example.

## Implementation & cautions

Disclose end time, conversion terms, cancellation and data retention before signup. Use authoritative time and deduplicated payment events in production. A browser day counter demonstrates policy only; repeated upgrade clicks do not represent repeated charges.
