## Why: the goal or problem

Imagine a transit screen with routes and times. Travelers scan quickly, but a typeface category alone cannot prove similar labels remain distinct.

## How: work toward a solution

For a train information board, compare a phrase with iiiWWW 0123 and aligned numbers. Edit the specimen, adjust its size, and show measured width guides or tabular digits. Text remains shaped as one string. Reset or reload restores the initial text at 48px. Change weight to compare the same glyphs at different strengths.

## What: the concept

**Sans serif** — Sans-serif letters omit serifs, but their terminals and proportions still vary.

Check ambiguous glyphs and localized CJK subsets instead of assuming universal readability.
