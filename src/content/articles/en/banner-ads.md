## Concept

Banner ads occupy a bounded region while the main app remains visible. Format describes placement, not a guaranteed revenue level or targeting method.

## Example

A fictional puzzle app shows its board above a labeled bottom banner, separated from game controls. The static diagram preserves the play area instead of covering it. No ad is requested and no reward is granted. Ad size, fill, revenue and network charges are intentionally outside this example.

## When to choose it

Choose it when a reserved region fits the screen. Keep controls distinct and compare interruptions from interstitial ads.
