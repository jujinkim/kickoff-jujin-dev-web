## Why: the goal or problem

A public cycling tool helps riders find quieter routes. Volunteers improve its openly licensed code. They want a nonprofit free-software community to operate collaboration instead of maintaining their own server.

## How: work toward a solution

Publish an eligible project on Codeberg. A contributor forks it, proposes a route-display fix and opens a pull request. Project maintainers review and merge; Codeberg’s operators maintain the hosting service.

## What: the concept

Codeberg is a nonprofit community service powered by Forgejo and built around Git. Gitea is software you can operate yourself. Check project eligibility and resource policies; community hosting is not a promise of unlimited resources.
