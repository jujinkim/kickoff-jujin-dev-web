## Why: the goal or problem

A photo-walk map needs controls over images without hiding the route. A solid block removes that context.

## How: work toward a solution

For the photo walk, track three tasks and two notes. Mark one task: progress becomes 1 / 3 and 33%. Filter tasks or open notes; reset restores the initial state. Opaque panels replace the frosted surfaces when selected.

## What: the concept

**Glassmorphism** — Translucent panels reveal a blurred background.

Contrast depends on the backdrop; offer an opaque mode and test each scene.
