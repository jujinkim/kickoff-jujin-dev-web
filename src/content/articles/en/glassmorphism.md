## Why: the goal or problem

A photo-walk planner helps walkers choose a route while viewing the landscape. Solid cards hide the scenery. Keep context behind a broad, stable frosted panel. Compact morphing controls would better suit browsing photos without a large information card.

## How: work toward a solution

Select the lake or pine route. Distance, duration and description change inside a frosted panel while the photograph remains visible. Switch to opaque panels to read without transparency. Reset returns to the lake route.

## What: the concept

[**Glassmorphism**](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/glassmorphism/) uses translucent surfaces, background blur and edge treatment to suggest glass. Blur alone does not ensure contrast. Test the lightest and darkest backdrops and provide an opaque fallback. Panel shape is an example choice.
