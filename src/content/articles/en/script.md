## Concept

Script type borrows handwritten strokes and possible joins. This editorial category is related to CSS cursive, not programming scripts.

## Example

Enter a Latin invitation heading in Dancing Script and adjust its size. Clearing the field leaves the heading empty; ordinary body text stays readable. Reset or reload restores the initial state.

## When to choose it

Choose it for short expressive headings. Long passages and unsupported characters need a plain fallback; this specimen demonstrates Latin lettering only.
