## Why: the goal or problem

Imagine a dinner invitation with place and time. Its short heading needs a handwritten greeting, but ordinary body lettering cannot create it.

## How: work toward a solution

The dinner invitation uses “You are invited” in Dancing Script, with place and time in plain text. Explore the separate measurement lab without breaking the string into character boxes. Localized Korean and Japanese use a Noto fallback.

## What: the concept

Script type borrows handwritten strokes and possible joins.

Long passages and unsupported characters need a plain fallback; this specimen demonstrates Latin lettering only.
