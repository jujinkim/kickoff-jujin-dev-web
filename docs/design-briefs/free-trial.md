# Free trial visualization brief

- Stable ID, leaf category, English/Korean/Japanese titles: `free-trial`; `access-strategies`; Free trial / 무료 체험 / 無料トライアル.
- Definition (one sentence): A free trial grants temporary access to evaluate a paid offering. Its duration and conversion policy must be stated; automatic charging is not inherent to every trial.
- Closest concept and concrete difference: same-category peers freemium; Time-limited evaluation. Cross-category combinations are related reading, not comparison peers.
- Distinguishing visual features (structure, material, typography): Prominent deadline panel above expiring feature rights. Solid surfaces and text/number labels; color is not the only encoding.
- Shared comparison category: [access-strategies](../catalog-writing/groups/access-strategies.md).
- Fixed scenario, dataset, labels, actions, and initial state inherited from that group: A notebook with editing and PDF export; day 0 of a manually advanced three-day clock, no payment method and no automatic conversion. Free editing remains in freemium; both features expire in the trial.
- Visual variable changed; concept-specific extra controls and their justification: Prominent deadline panel above expiring feature rights. Controls expose the mechanism described below; no background timer or real checkout.
- Fictional scenario and why it demonstrates the definition: A fictional notebook starts a three-day trial with editing and PDF export. Advance days manually: at day three both lock.
