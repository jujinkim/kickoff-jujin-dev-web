# Consumable purchase visualization brief

- Stable ID, leaf category, English/Korean/Japanese titles: `consumable-purchase`; `purchase-types`; Consumable purchase / 소모성 구매 / 消耗型購入.
- Definition (one sentence): A consumable purchase supplies units that are depleted through use and can be bought again. It differs from a non-consumable feature entitlement.

- Distinct familiar situation, dataset, labels, actions, and initial state for this article: A fictional crossword app starts with zero hints.
- Visual variable changed; concept-specific extra controls and their justification: Puzzle board beside depleting hint inventory. Controls expose the mechanism described below; no background timer or real checkout.
- Fictional scenario and why it demonstrates the definition: A crossword player uses one hint now and may need another later; an unlimited unlock would not track that spending. A fictional crossword app starts with zero hints.
- Representative action and observable result: A fictional crossword app starts with zero hints. Buy three, then use hints repeatedly: the balance falls to zero and further use is blocked.
- Initial state: Zero hints, purchases and uses.
- Changed state, repeated action, empty input/no results: Where present, integer inputs accept 0–10,000 only; empty, negative and fractional values report an error without a charge. Insufficient balances never become negative. Access, purchase and ad actions are guarded; repeated non-consumable purchase and duplicate ad completion cannot grant extra rights or rewards.
