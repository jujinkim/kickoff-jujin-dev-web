# Styles / 스타일 / スタイル

Group ID: `styles` · Checked: 2026-09-21 · Writing designs, not article bodies.

## Shared case and comparison contract

A fictional reading card: title Field notes, summary Three ways to save an article, metadata 5 min, Save button, Saved state, and visible focus. Keep content and action identical across seven studies.

**Comparison axes, fixed order:** surface and depth; ornament; information retained; action signifiers; background dependence; state visibility.

**Boundary:** Overlapping design labels, not standards or exclusive categories. No single inventor, mandatory palette or conversion claim. Minimalism can accompany any surface treatment; layout and font width remain independent.

**Visual family:** Annotated screen studies: neutral reference and style variant. Show default, focus and saved states. Label changed tokens. These are fictional studies, not service screenshots.

**Core terms (EN / KO / JA):** Surface / 표면 / 表面; Signifier / 행동 단서 / シグニファイア; Visual hierarchy / 시각적 위계 / 視覚的階層.

## Cross-review decisions

Keep the fixture, comparison-axis order and five frontmatter dimensions consistent across siblings. A benefit in one row must not imply an unqualified defect in all other rows. Do not confuse implementation, abstraction, presentation and commercial responsibility. The scope boundary above resolves overlapping choices; combinations are explicitly permitted in the last column.

Review every English draft in this group together before translation. Confirm the figures preserve identical inputs and label changed assumptions. Reject unsupported speed, revenue, accessibility or popularity rankings. Translate the approved argument with the group terms, not by substituting product marketing language.

## Pilot design review — 2026-09-21

Reviewed all seven designs before extending this structure to other groups. Brutalism and neobrutalism share terminology, so their accounts are cross-read; no founder or exclusive taxonomy is asserted. Glass and soft extrusion use a second explanatory source. Skeuomorphism separates metaphor from realism. Flat design and minimalism separate surface depth from content selection. The same Save task survives all seven studies. These decisions form the baseline for the remaining groups; they are design review, not user-test results.
