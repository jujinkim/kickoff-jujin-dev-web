# Seller responsibility models / 판매 책임 모델 / 販売責任モデル

Group ID: `seller-responsibility` · Checked: 2026-09-21 · Writing designs, not article bodies.

## Shared case and comparison contract

Sell a fictional digital reading-tool license to a customer. Compare business as contractual seller using a payment processor versus a contracted MoR reseller handling the covered transaction. Track customer receipt, money settlement, refund routing and transaction-tax responsibility only within the illustrative arrangement.

**Comparison axes, fixed order:** contractual seller; payment handling; receipt identity; refund/dispute route; transaction-tax role; retained product obligations.

**Boundary:** Role allocation depends on contract, product and jurisdiction. A provider can offer both processor and MoR products. Outsourcing the transaction role does not erase product delivery, support, corporate tax or all legal obligations.

**Visual family:** Separate money (solid), contract (dashed) and responsibility (labeled ownership) diagrams with customer, business, processor and optional MoR.

**Core terms (EN / KO / JA):** Contractual seller / 계약상 판매자 / 契約上の販売者; Payment processor / 결제 처리업체 / 決済処理事業者; Settlement / 정산 / 精算.
