# Shared terminology / 공통 용어 / 共通用語

English is the writing language. Korean and Japanese columns fix editorial vocabulary for later translation; these are not article translations. Product names stay unchanged. Preserve candidate titles and IDs exactly. A technical term’s first occurrence should pair the local expression with English when ambiguity remains.

Keep **List** (collection) separate from **List layout** (visual arrangement), **Script** (handwriting-like type) from programming/writing-system senses, and **direct payment** (payer) from **direct seller** (responsibility). Serif/sans/cursive comparisons need actual multilingual glyph inspection; Latin labels are not universal CJK classification rules. MoR and sponsor translations do not establish legal status.
