# Interstitial ads / 전면 광고 / インタースティシャル広告

ID: `interstitial-ads` · Group: [ad-formats](../groups/ad-formats.md) · Checked: 2026-09-22.

## Scope and argument

Interstitial ads cover the app at a natural break, such as completing a level. They interrupt the screen flow, unlike a banner beside content.

A fictional puzzle app shows a numbered sequence: level complete, labeled full-screen ad, next level. The closing action is drawn as a label in this static diagram, not a working ad control. No ad is requested and no reward is promised. Timing, revenue and network charges are omitted.

Choose it only where a natural transition fits. Consider interruption costs and distinguish optional rewarded participation.

## Comparison and terminology

Peers: `banner-ads`, `rewarded-ads`. Five comparison fields: Full-screen transition placement; Uses a natural break; Interrupts app flow; Clear level or task boundaries; Advertising revenue.
