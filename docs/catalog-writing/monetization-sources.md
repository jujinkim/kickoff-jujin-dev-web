# Monetization source refresh

Inspected **2026-09-22** using official source pages. Sources establish definitions and mechanisms; all numeric fixtures, conversion outcomes, example policies and suitability advice are authored illustrations. No actual fees, revenue promises or jurisdiction-specific tax judgments. English groups reviewed before Korean/Japanese translation.

| Evidence                                                                                                                     | Inspected passage                                                                | Claim and boundary                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [checkout](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/checkout)                                                                        | Checkout overview                                                                | One-time and recurring payment collection; a payment UI alone does not define purchase rights.                                                                                               |
| [ads](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128738?hl=en)                                                                 | Available ad formats                                                             | Banner occupies a bounded region; interstitial fills the screen at natural transitions. Format is separate from ad content/type.                                                             |
| [sponsors](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/getting-started-with-github-sponsors/about-github-sponsors)                   | About GitHub Sponsors; sponsorship tiers                                         | Provider example of financial support, optional tiers, one-time and monthly support. No universal charitable/tax status.                                                                     |
| [affiliate](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement)                                                   | Sections 1, 3 and 5                                                              | Qualifying referrals/commissions, Amazon customer relationship and associate disclosure. No universal rate or guaranteed earnings.                                                           |
| [fees](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/marketplace/tasks/app-fees)                                                           | Collect fees when using destination charges; flow of funds                       | Platform application fees are distinct from processing fees. Our 100 = 90 + 10 is net allocation before omitted costs, not Stripe transfer chronology or profit.                             |
| [subscription](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/overview)                                                       | Subscription lifecycle; subscription statuses; cancellation                      | Recurring invoices and state changes; access handling is an application policy. Immediate suspension on failure and end-of-paid-period cancellation are this demo’s policies.                |
| [usage](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/usage-based)                                                                         | Usage-based billing opening                                                      | Charges follow measured consumption. No Metronome integration or vendor price claims. Base-plus-overage mechanism also appears in the pricing-model index.                                   |
| [credits](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/usage-based/billing-credits)                                         | Billing credits overview and supported use cases                                 | Prepaid billing credit concept. The demo’s integer export credits, immediate blocking and no expiry are authored policies, not a claim that Stripe grants behave identically.                |
| [mor](https://stripe.com/resources/more/merchant-of-record)                                                                  | What is a merchant of record?; responsibilities; payment facilitator distinction | Contractual transaction seller vs processor; retained product duties. Diagrams assume a contract and do not prescribe country tax law or coverage.                                           |
| [pricing](https://docs.stripe.com/products-prices/pricing-models)                                                            | Recurring pricing models table; flat rate and per-seat linked pages              | Package amount, licensed quantity and usage are distinct axes. Feature-tiered packages are editorial taxonomy, separate from quantity tiers.                                                 |
| [tiers](https://docs.stripe.com/subscriptions/pricing-models/tiered-pricing)                                                 | Volume-based pricing; graduated pricing; flat fee examples                       | Volume uses final rate for all units; graduated sums slices. Our zero-usage result assumes no tier flat fee. 100/101 boundary demonstrates volume total discontinuity.                       |
| [apple](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/business-models/)                                                              | Freemium; in-app purchase types                                                  | Ongoing free use with optional paid enhancements; no claim that conversion is automatic.                                                                                                     |
| [trial](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/trials)                                                                | Trial periods; trials without collecting payment details                         | Limited evaluation and configurable end behavior. Our three-day clock and explicit paid choice are fictional, not a required provider default.                                               |
| [iap](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/in-app-purchases-and-subscriptions/in-app-purchase-types) | Consumable and Non-Consumable rows                                               | Consumable units deplete; non-consumable rights do not expire/decrease with use. Reset clears the simulation, not a real entitlement.                                                        |
| [reward](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/7313578?hl=en)                                                              | Rewards requirements 1–2; implementation requirements 1–3                        | Non-transferable in-app benefit, disclosed condition, affirmative opt-in, dismissal without impeding normal use, reward on completion. Demo has no ad SDK or production reward verification. |

Additional pricing passages inspected: [flat-rate packages](https://docs.stripe.com/subscriptions/pricing-models/flat-rate-pricing), [per-seat quantities](https://docs.stripe.com/subscriptions/pricing-models/per-seat-pricing).
